## 4. Technical Notes for Design Review & Assembly

### 4.1. `SFU1605` vs. `DFU1605` Backlash Strategy & Future Upgrade Path (`3X`, `3Y`)
* **Standard Single-Nut `SFU1605` (Selected Base Option):** Costs 2.5× less than `DFU1605` and has a compact `48 mm` nut length, preserving the full `>200 mm` travel stroke on a `400 mm` screw. Rolled C7 single nuts exhibit `30–50 µm` of axial backlash upon direction reversal. In our firmware, this is mitigated at zero hardware cost via **unidirectional approach positioning** (always approaching target lithography coordinates from the same direction) and software backlash compensation in `Raspberry Pi Pico 2 H`.
* **Factory Double-Nut `DFU1605`:** Eliminates axial play mechanically using an internal spacer shim between two nut halves, but increases nut length to `85 mm` (reducing stroke by `37 mm`) and significantly increases cost.
* **Future Preload Upgrade Without Replacing the Screw (Item #12):** Because both `SFU1605` and `DFU1605` use the exact same `Ø16 mm / 5 mm lead` rolled shaft (`3.175 mm` ball diameter), we can upgrade any axis later by ordering a standalone `SFU1605` nut (`227.48 CZK bez DPH` on AliExpress or `500.00 CZK bez DPH` at RStechnic.cz) and preloading it against the opposite face of the `DSG16H` housing using a Belleville spring washer (`DIN 2093`).

### 4.2. Vertical Stack-Up Alignment: `SBR16` Rails vs. `BK12 + DSG16H` (`2.0 mm` Shim)
* The combined height of a supported rail `SBR16` and carriage block `SBR16UU` (`1X+2X`, `1Y+2Y`) from the mounting plate to the underside of the carried stage is **`45.0 mm`**.
* The centerline height of fixed bearing `BK12` (`B_X, B_Y`) is `25.0 mm`, and the distance from the screw centerline to the top mounting face of nut housing `DSG16H` (`3X, 3Y`) is `22.0 mm`, totaling **`47.0 mm`**.
* **Assembly Action:** Place standard **`2.0 mm` thick M5 flat washers (`DIN 125 / DIN 433`)** between the top of the `SBR16UU` carriage blocks (`2X, 2Y`) and the underside of plates `4X, 4Y`. This brings both the rail stack and the ball-screw stack to an identical **`47.0 mm`** plane, preventing screw binding at the ends of travel.

### 4.3. Fastener Specification (`DIN 912` Socket Screws & `DIN 934` Hex Nuts for `8 mm` Plates)
* **Supported Rails `SBR16` (`1X, 1Y`):** `32× M5×20 mm DIN 912` screws + `32× M5` washers + `32× M5 DIN 934` hex nuts (`nut height = 4.0 mm`).
* **Carriage Blocks `SBR16UU` (`2X, 2Y`):** `32× M5×16 mm DIN 912` screws + `32× M5` leveling washers (`2.0 mm` thickness) threaded directly into the `M5` tapped blind holes (`10 mm` thread depth) in the carriage blocks.
* **Bearing Supports `BK12 / BF12` (`B_X, B_Y`):** `8× M6×25 mm DIN 912` screws + `8× M6 DIN 934` hex nuts (`nut height = 5.0 mm`).
* **Ball Nut Housings `DSG16H` (`3X, 3Y`):** `8× M6×20 mm DIN 912` screws threaded into the housing.
* **Stepper Motors `NEMA23` (`M_X, M_Y`):** `8× M5×25 mm DIN 912` screws + `8× M5 DIN 934` hex nuts (`nut height = 4.0 mm`).

### 4.4. Electrical Safety: `33.0 V` Bus Adjustment & `3.3 V` Optical Endstop Interfacing
* **`BTT TMC5160 V1.3` Capacitor Margin:** Although the `TMC5160-TA` IC is rated up to `60 V`, the onboard SMD electrolytic decoupling capacitors on the `V1.3 / T V1.0` StepStick module are rated at **`35 V`**. Before connecting the drivers, turn the `Vadj` potentiometer on the `Mean Well LRS-150-36` power supply counter-clockwise to set the DC bus to **`33.0 V`** (verified with a multimeter).
* **SPI Current Limiting:** Configure the `IHOLD_IRUN` register over SPI to **`1.6–1.8 A RMS`** (`IHOLD = 40%`). With a `5 mm` lead ball screw, `1.6 A` yields `>100 kgf` (`~1 000 N`) of linear thrust—more than 100× the force required to move the horizontal wafer stage—while keeping driver and motor temperatures near ambient.
* **Optical Endstop `TCST2103` (`LS_X-, LS_X+, LS_Y-, LS_Y+`):** The `RP2350` MCU on `Raspberry Pi Pico 2 H` uses **strictly `3.3 V` GPIO pins** (not `5 V` tolerant!). Drive the `TCST2103` infrared emitter LED from the `5.0 V` buck converter rail through a **`180–220 Ω` resistor**, and wire the phototransistor collector pull-up resistor (**`10 kΩ`**) **strictly to the `3V3(OUT)` pin** on `Raspberry Pi Pico 2 H`.
