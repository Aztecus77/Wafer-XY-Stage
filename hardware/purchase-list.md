## 3. Technical Notes for Design Review & Assembly

### 3.1. `BS-1605` / `SF-1605-4` vs. Double-Nut `DF-1605-4` Backlash Strategy & Required End Machining (`1c`, `3X`, `3Y`)
* **Single Preloaded Nut `SF-1605-4` (Selected Base Option):** Costs less than half the price of a double nut (`500.00 CZK ex. VAT` vs. `1 100.00 CZK ex. VAT`) and has a compact `48 mm` body length, preserving the full `>200 mm` travel stroke on a `400 mm` screw. Residual reversal play (`~20–40 µm`) is compensated in firmware via **unidirectional coordinate approach** and software backlash compensation on `Raspberry Pi Pico 2 H`.
* **Future Mechanical Preload Upgrade:** Because the rolled `BS-1605` shaft (`Ø16 mm`, `5 mm` lead, `3.175 mm` ball diameter) is identical for single and double nuts, a second `SF-1605-4` nut can be added later and tensioned against the opposite face of the `DSG16H` housing (`1h`) via a Belleville spring washer (`DIN 2093`), or replaced with RStechnic's factory double nut [`DF-1605-4`](https://www.rstechnic.cz/Matice-dvojita-pro-kulickovy-sroub-prumer-16-mm-stoupani-5-mm-predepnuta-verze-d86.htm).
* **Required Lathe End Machining of `BS-1605` (`1c`):** Meter-cut ball screws (`BS-1605`) ship with raw abrasive-cut ends (`Ø16 mm` full thread). Before assembly into `BK-12` (`1f`), `BF-12` (`1g`), and the `8×10 mm` coupler (`1i`), machine both ends of each `400 mm` screw on a lathe to standard `BK12 / BF12` journal tolerances:
  * **Driven End (`BK-12` + Coupler):** Turn a **`Ø12.00 mm (h7)`** bearing journal, cut an **`M12×1.0`** locknut thread, and turn a **`Ø10.00 mm (h7)`** output shaft (`15 mm` length) for the `10 mm` bore of the `8×10 mm` coupler (`1i`).
  * **Floating End (`BF-12`):** Turn a **`Ø10.00 mm (h7)`** bearing journal with a circlip groove (`DIN 471`).

### 3.2. Vertical Stack-Up Alignment: `SBR-16 + SME-16L` vs. `BK-12 + DSG16H` (`2.0 mm` Shim)
* The combined height of a supported rail `SBR-16` and extended carriage block `SME-16L` (`1X+2X`, `1Y+2Y`) from the base plate to the underside of the carried plate is **`45.0 mm`**.
* The shaft centerline height of fixed bearing `BK-12` (`B_X, B_Y`) is `25.0 mm`, and the distance from the screw centerline to the top mounting face of nut housing `DSG16H` (`1h`, `3X, 3Y`) is `22.0 mm`, totaling **`47.0 mm`**.
* **Assembly Action:** Insert standard **`2.0 mm` thick M5 flat washers (`DIN 125 / DIN 433`)** between the top mounting surface of the `SME-16L` carriage blocks (`2X, 2Y`) and the underside of plates `4X, 4Y` so both assemblies lie on an exact **`47.0 mm`** plane.

### 3.3. Electrical Safety: `33.0 V` PSU Adjustment, `HW-636` Pre-Calibration & `3.3 V` Optical Endstops
* **`BTT TMC5160 V1.3` Voltage Margin (`33.0 V`):** Although the `TMC5160` chip handles up to `60 V`, the onboard SMD decoupling capacitors on the `V1.3` module are rated for **`35 V`**. Before connecting the drivers, turn the `Vadj` potentiometer on the `Mean Well LRS-150-36` power supply counter-clockwise to set the DC output to **`33.0 V`**.
* **`HW-636` Buck Converter Pre-Calibration (`5.0 V`):** The `HW-636` (`SCT2650`) module accepts up to `60 V` on its input, but its secondary output filter capacitors are rated at `35 V` and the factory trimmer pot may be set high. First power the `HW-636` from a `<24 V` bench supply, adjust the output potentiometer to **`5.0 V`** with a multimeter, and only then connect it to the `33.0 V` main bus and `Pico 2 H` (`VSYS`, rated `1.8–5.5 V`).
* **SPI Current Limiting (`1.6–1.8 A RMS`):** Program the `IHOLD_IRUN` register over SPI to **`1.6–1.8 A RMS`** (`IHOLD = 40%`). On a `5 mm` lead ball screw, `1.6 A` produces `>100 kgf` (`~1 000 N`) of linear thrust while drawing only `~0.35–0.50 A` per axis from the `33.0 V` power supply (`~35–40 W` total system power vs. `154.8 W` PSU capacity).
* **Optical Endstop `TCST2103` (`LS_X-, LS_X+, LS_Y-, LS_Y+`):** The `RP2350` MCU on `Raspberry Pi Pico 2 H` uses **strictly `3.3 V` GPIO pins**. Feed the `TCST2103` IR emitter LED from the `5.0 V` rail through a **`180–220 Ω` resistor** (`~17 mA`), and wire the phototransistor collector pull-up resistor (**`10 kΩ`**) **strictly to the `3V3(OUT)` pin** on `Raspberry Pi Pico 2 H`.
